# eShop

A small online store where Shoppers browse Products and buy them. This glossary fixes the words used for Favourites.

## Language

**Product**:
An item for sale in the catalog.
_Avoid_: Catalog item, SKU, listing

**Shopper**:
A person using the store, signed in or not.
_Avoid_: User, customer, buyer

**Favourite**:
A Product a signed-in Shopper has saved to come back to later.
_Avoid_: Wishlist, bookmark, liked item, saved item
